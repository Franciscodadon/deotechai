Meet the Founder

An operator first. Technology came second, because the business needed it.

I did not come from a technical background. I came into technology because I needed better systems to compete and grow in real estate. That curiosity turned into a strength for spotting friction, connecting workflows, and simplifying complex processes. After helping others improve their CRM, follow-up, and automation, I realized the bigger opportunity was not just using technology better, but helping business owners understand what is working, what is not, and where to focus next.

Technology should create clarity, leverage, and freedom, not more complexity.

Today, I am building Deo Tech AI around a simple belief: technology should create clarity, leverage, and freedom, not more complexity. I approach AI from the perspective of an operator and business owner, with a focus on customer journeys, KPIs, payments, and the places where automation can improve performance without removing the human touch. My goal is to help leaders make better decisions, build stronger systems, and spend more time working on the business instead of inside it.

That mission is personal. I was named after my grandfather, an entrepreneur and restaurant owner in Honduras, and I have always believed entrepreneurship is about building something that can outlast you. Deo Tech AI is being built to help founders, young entrepreneurs, and organizations develop the AI fluency and operating intelligence they need to create lasting impact, more freedom, and a legacy their families can be proud of.
